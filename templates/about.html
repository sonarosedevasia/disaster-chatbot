Official Legal & Public Safety Disclaimer

DisasterSafe is developed as an educational disaster management and decision-support tool. It implements standard operating procedures outlined by NDMA, KSDMA, IMD, and CWC. It is NOT a substitute for professional first responders, search-and-rescue teams, fire services, or lawful mandatory evacuation directives issued by District Magistrates or the State Relief Commissioner.

Under active life-or-death crisis conditions, prioritize calling 112 immediately. The developers assume no legal liability for local variations in disaster impact.
